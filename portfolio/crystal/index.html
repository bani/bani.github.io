Crystal Crater

2026

A hub world for VRChat that links the other worlds in this portfolio: a sunken crystal garden inside an alien crater, with the glowing Heart Crystal at its centre and six portal alcoves opening off a ring path like split geodes. Each alcove is colour-coded by theme (violet for meditation, green for gardens, ice-blue for sci-fi) and holds a real object from the world it leads to. Above the rim, a ringed gas giant, a moon, a comet and a nebula fill the night sky, and a small cave in the crater wall opens, impossibly, onto a vast canyon.
