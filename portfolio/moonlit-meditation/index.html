Moonlit Meditation

2026

A VRChat world for guided group meditation: a candlelit hall on a glacial lake at night, under a low moon and the Milky Way. Wool cushions fan out from the leader's seat on a small walnut stage, and the glass wall behind the leader frames the moon over the mountain.

The hall was generated with Python scripts in Blender, and the cushions were arranged so that every seat has a clear view of the leader. The mountains, forest and lake are the Lakeside Lounge valley re-lit for night and rendered in Cycles as a single panorama, with the moon and the Milky Way placed in the sky by calibration renders.

Candles, paper andon lamps and a warm wash along the oak ceiling are all baked into the lighting. Several features are available for the session leader, such as video, pens, bell sound, mute the room and control stage voice.
