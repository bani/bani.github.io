Herbal Tea Bottle

2026

A product render of a glass bottle for a herbal tea brand with a playful identity. Working from that brief, I modelled the bottle in the shape of a tea cup, with a handle and an off-center pouring nozzle.

The technical focus was material work under a single lighting setup: glass with accurate refraction and transparency, metal hardware, and liquid visible through the bottle walls. Glass was the hardest to control, since it picks up reflections and colour from everything around it. The project was delivered as a full branded product mockup with packaging.
