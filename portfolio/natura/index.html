Natura Nexus

2026

A biophilic office world for VRChat, built for meetings and gatherings rather than desk work. Every room opens off the Grove, a sunlit garden atrium with trees and moving water under a timber lattice roof: a reception with a living green wall, a café and terrace, six meeting rooms each facing a pocket garden, a library lounge with a fireplace, and the Forum, a sunken oak-lined hall for 50. Limestone, oak, plaster and wool keep the palette calm, and soft late-morning daylight from the wooded hillside outside fills every room.
