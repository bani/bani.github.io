Nebula Nexus

2026

A cozy and whimsical event venue for VR, built on the same plan as Natura Nexus: reception, a 50-seat auditorium, six meeting rooms named after moons, a lounge, a café and a central atrium. Indigo floors, orchid walls and walnut slat ceilings are traced with warm champagne and amber light lines, arches soften every corner, and the atrium's hanging orrery glows above a round pool.
